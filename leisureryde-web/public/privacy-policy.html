LeisureRyde ("we") operates the LeisureRyde mobile application, which connects riders with independent drivers for on-demand transportation. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Who we are

Leisure Ryde Enterprises, LLC, registered at 1027 Park Hollow Way Lawrenceville, GA 30043 United States, is the data controller responsible for your personal information under this policy.

How we use your information

  • To match riders with available drivers and facilitate trips
  • To calculate fares and process payments
  • To show real-time driver and rider location during an active trip
  • To enable in-app communication between a matched rider and driver
  • To send trip status notifications (e.g. "Your driver has arrived")
  • To maintain ride history and support customer service requests
  • To detect fraud and maintain the safety of our platform

How we share information

We share limited information as needed to operate the service:

  • Between matched riders and drivers — name, photo, rating, phone number (for calling), live location, and chat messages, only once a trip is matched.
  • Service providers — Firebase (Google) for authentication, data storage, and push notifications; Stripe for payment processing; Google Maps Platform for routing and geocoding. These providers process data on our behalf and are contractually restricted from using it for their own purposes.
  • Legal compliance — if required by law, court order, or to protect the safety of our users.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Location data

Riders' location is collected when actively using the app to request a ride. Drivers' location is collected continuously while they are online and available for trips, including while the app is in the background, so that riders can see an accurate, real-time arrival estimate and the platform can match nearby drivers efficiently.

Drivers can stop all location collection at any time by switching to "Offline" in the app. Background location tracking ends immediately when a driver goes offline.

Payments

All card payments are processed directly by Stripe, Inc. We do not receive or store full card numbers, expiry dates, or CVC codes. We retain a record of completed transactions (amount, date, trip reference) for accounting and fare dispute purposes.

Data retention

We retain account and trip data for as long as your account is active. After you delete your account, we delete associated personal data within 40 days, except where we are required to retain certain records (such as completed transaction records) for tax, accounting, or legal compliance purposes, in which case we retain only what is legally necessary and for no longer than required.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or to object to certain processing. You can:

  • Edit your profile information directly within the app
  • Delete your account and associated data at any time from Account > Delete Account within the app
  • Request account deletion without the app installed via our Account Deletion page
  • Contact us at the email below for any other privacy request

Security

All data transmitted between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (TLS/HTTPS). Data stored in our backend (Firebase Firestore) is encrypted at rest by our infrastructure provider. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's privacy

LeisureRyde is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by updating the "Last updated" date above, and where appropriate, through an in-app notice.
